P2P Messenger is a private, decentralised, end-to-end encrypted messenger. Messages are encrypted on your device and sent <b>directly</b> to your contact over WebRTC. There is no central server that stores messages, accounts or phone numbers.

<b>Features</b>
* Identity without registration: no email, phone number or SMS. Your identity is a did:key created on the device.
* Recovery with 12 words (BIP39).
* Add a contact with a QR code or a short invitation code.
* 12 verification words, computed from both public keys, to check that nobody is in the middle.
* Encrypted chats (Double Ratchet). History and contacts are stored on the device, encrypted with AES-GCM.
* Files, photos, location (sent only when you tap) and deleting a chat for both sides.
* Audio and video calls over the same direct connection.
* Groups of up to 9 people.
* Bulgarian and English interface, light and dark theme.

<b>Network</b>
The app has no push notifications and no Google services. Contacts connect directly. To find each other over the internet it can use a small signalling service that only relays short signed invitations and cannot read messages. A public STUN server is used for NAT traversal. No TURN relay: on some networks a direct connection is not possible.

The source code is open (MIT licence).
